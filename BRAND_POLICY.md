# Brand policy

The `Corral` name and the floral-hat artwork (including the README display
image in `docs/brand/`) identify this project. They are **not** covered by the
code license in `LICENSE`, and no trademark registration is asserted here.

## Forks and distributions

- Do not present your fork or build as `Corral`, and do not ship it under the
  Corral name, icon, or other artwork. Pick a distinct name and distinct
  artwork for anything you distribute.
- Descriptive references are fine: you may truthfully say things like
  "compatible with the Corral relay protocol" or "a fork of corral-relay" in
  plain text, without using the artwork or implying endorsement.

## What stays untouched

- Assistant-provider and platform identities (Claude, Codex, Cursor, OpenCode,
  Pi, Apple, GitHub, and their marks) keep their own official identities.
  Nothing in this policy grants their use beyond nominative reference.
- Product-fact claims in this repository must stay honest: do not advertise
  shared hosted relays, store builds, or binaries that do not exist.
